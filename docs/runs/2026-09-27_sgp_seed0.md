## 4. Attribution and reported contrasts (secondary)

- **Adaptive-memory attribution: `strong`.** A − P on Object after Goal is **+16 pp [+6, +26]**.
  The ne90 memory still helps under SGP, and by the end the gap reaches +30 pp on Object and +52 pp
  on Goal.
- **Scaled-projection attribution: `directional`.**
  - g = 0.36: A recovers about a third of adaptive GPM's T4 probe-loss gap.
  - There is no rollout-level gain: A − N at T4 is +2 pp [−8, +12].
- **The interaction `(A − P) − (N − G)` is strongly negative.**

## 5. Mechanism — measured, not causal

The importances at each stage are medians over layers, from `importance_summary` in the report:

| After | P, trunk λ / share of λ = 1 | P, decoder | A, trunk | A, decoder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 0.77 / 0.4% | 0.82 / 4.5% | (= P) | (= P) |
| T2 | 1.0 / 83% | 1.0 / 89% | 1.0 / 57% | 0.63 / 33% |
| T3 | 1.0 / 92% | 1.0 / 96% | 1.0 / 79% | 0.46 / 36% |
| T4 | 1.0 / 94% | 1.0 / 92% | 1.0 / 93% | 0.50 / 30% |

1. **During T2, T1's directions are only softly protected.** After T1, almost no basis has λ = 1:
   Eq. 2 gives exactly 1 only to each layer's top direction. With α = 25 the median is about 0.8, so
   about 20% of each T1 component of the update still reaches the weights. This coincides with the
   44-pp loss of Spatial at T1 → T2.
2. **The SGP baseline then hardens quickly.** Eq. 10 accumulates importance, and from T2 on 83–96%
   of its bases have λ = 1. From there it behaves much like plain GPM, which is consistent with P ≈ G
   on the later cells and with g = 0.97.
3. **Adaptive SGP's added directions stay soft.** The ne90 target adds low-energy tail directions,
   the ones that protected Object under adaptive GPM. Eq. 2 gives them low importance by design: the
   decoder's median λ is about 0.5, and only about 30–36% of its bases are fully protected.
   - Object's probe loss after Goal is 0.066 under A, against 0.023 under N and 0.089 under G.
   - This matches the negative interaction: SGP's scaling removes much of the protection that made
     adaptive GPM work.

These are associations at one seed. The causal statements this record makes are only the paired
contrasts in §3–4.

## 6. What this does and does not show
- **It shows**, for α = 25, the fixed ε = 0.95 memory and our AdamW coupling, on seed 0:
  - basis-wise scaled projection loses T1 at the first transition;
  - under adaptive memory it gives back most of adaptive GPM's Object retention;
  - it recovers only about a third of the T4 probe-loss gap.
- **It does not show** that scaled projection cannot work in this setting:
  - α sets how soft the protection is (§5, points 1 and 3);
  - larger α moves SGP towards GPM;
  - the accumulation rule and the ε schedule are also paper choices we fixed.

  Only one α has been run.
- **The registered claim for α = 25 is settled at level 0.** Seeds 1–2 could only show whether the
  failure is consistent.
