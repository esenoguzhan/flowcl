# SGP at α = 800, seed 0 — an exploratory four-task look at saturation

**Status: exploratory, no claim level.**
- α = 800 lies outside the registered bracket ({25, 100, 400, 1600}), and no α passed held-out
  validation (`docs/runs/2026-09-29_sgp_sensitivity.md`).
- The registered SGP rule's gates and objectives are reused here as **descriptive labels** only.
- There is one seed and no replication.

**The descriptive label for adaptive SGP is `retention_only`.**
- **O1 is met.** Object after Goal is 70% against plain GPM's 2%: **+68 pp [+54, +80]**.
- **O2 is not met.** g = **−0.63** (batch CI −0.78 to −0.51), and at least 0.5 was required. T3 and
  T4 success clear their thresholds.
- **Every validity gate passes:** identity, implementation, the premise and the plasticity gap.

**In short:**
- **The SGP baseline at α = 800 behaves like plain GPM.**
  - Its rollouts are close to plain GPM's everywhere, with the same F₁ (0.585).
  - α = 25's loss of Spatial at T1 → T2 is gone (Spatial after Object 94% against 50%).
- **Adaptive SGP at α = 800 reproduces adaptive GPM's retention at the transition, but not at the
  end.**
  - Object after Goal: 70% against 68% (A − N +2 [−12, +16]).
  - Object at the end: 64% against 84% (A − N **−20 [−36, −4]**).
- **It does not recover T4 plasticity. It makes it worse.**
  - T4 probe loss is 0.0166, against adaptive GPM's 0.0135 and plain GPM's 0.0085.
  - The T4 training loss agrees (0.0171 against 0.0137).
  - The rollout difference at T4 is not significant (88% against 92%, −4 [−14, +6]).
- **Measured, not causal:** during T4, 87% of AdamW's raw trunk step (by norm) lies inside the
  memory, against 22% under adaptive GPM.
  - The post-step projection then removes most of those components, since almost all of them have
    λ close to or equal to 1.
  - This is consistent with the optimizer-coupling difference found in the sensitivity study (§4.2
    there).

## 5. Mechanism — measured, not causal

**The importances.** Medians over layers of λ, and the share with λ = 1, from `importance_summary`
in the report. The memory after T*k* is the one in force while T*k*+1 trains.

| After | P800, trunk λ / share of λ = 1 | P800, decoder | A800, trunk | A800, decoder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 0.99 / 0.4% | 0.99 / 4.5% | (= P800) | (= P800) |
| T2 | 1.0 / 87% | 1.0 / 83% | 1.0 / 60% | 0.98 / 34% |
| T3 | 1.0 / 91% | 1.0 / 95% | 1.0 / 81% | 1.0 / 51% |
| T4 | 1.0 / 94% | 1.0 / 94% | 1.0 / 94% | 1.0 / 66% |

**Where AdamW's step goes.** The table gives `update_c`, the norm fraction of AdamW's raw step
(before the post-step projection) inside the memory, `‖ΔW M‖ / ‖ΔW‖`. Each value is the layer
median, averaged over the logged steps. The trunk is shown; the decoder follows the same pattern.

| Training | G | N | P25 | A25 | P800 | A800 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T2 | 0.09 | 0.09 | 0.89 | 0.89 | 0.89 | 0.89 |
| T3 | 0.09 | 0.14 | 0.52 | 0.86 | 0.48 | 0.83 |
| T4 | 0.09 | 0.22 | 0.42 | 0.91 | 0.44 | **0.87** |

**Memory occupancy** (the trunk's median ρ, in force during T4): A800 0.92 and N 0.94, against
P800 0.56 and G 0.58. The shared memory rule fills the trunk the same way under both projections.

**Reading:**
1. **Eq. 10 saturates the importances quickly at α = 800.**
   - After T1, the median λ is already 0.99, although almost no basis is exactly hard.
   - From T2 on, the accumulation `min(1, λ + λ_τ)` makes most trunk bases hard: 60–94% under A800
     and 87–94% under P800.
   - A800's decoder stays softer (34–66% hard), where the ne90 target adds its low-energy
     directions.
   - So at this α, SGP's soft scaling applies to few trunk directions, and the median λ is close
     to 1 everywhere.
2. **Adam still sees the soft components, and they dominate its step as the memory fills.**
   - Under GPM, every memory component of the gradient is removed before AdamW. Its raw step lies
     only 9–22% inside the memory, re-introduced by Adam's coordinate-wise normalisation, and the
     post-step projection removes that part.
   - Under SGP, only the hard bases are removed before AdamW. With the adaptive memory nearly full
     (trunk ρ 0.92 during T4), 87% of AdamW's raw step lies inside the memory. The post-step
     projection then scales each of those components by `1 − λ`, which is small or zero.
   - Under P800, whose memory is about half full, the share is 44%, and its T4 loss is close to
     G's (g_P = 0.87).
3. **This coincides with a worse T4 fit.** A800's T4 training and probe losses are about 25% above
   adaptive GPM's. This is consistent with the free directions receiving a smaller share of each
   normalised step.
4. **So at high α, our SGP does not approach GPM.** It approaches GPM with a less effective
   optimizer step, and the difference grows as the memory saturates.
   - That is where SGP was meant to help.
   - The sensitivity study found the same coupling difference at T1 → T2 (§4.2 there), where it
     cost little plasticity.
5. **At α = 25, the softer λ passed more of the update.** A25's T4 loss (0.0117) is below N's, but it
   lost Object: 30% at the end, against 84%.
   - Neither α recovers the plasticity gap while keeping adaptive GPM's retention.

These are associations at one seed, across runs that differ in more than one respect. The only causal
statements this record makes are the paired contrasts in §3–4. The coupling reading would need a
run that changes only the coupling.

## 6. What this does and does not show

- **It shows**, for α = 800, the fixed ε = 0.95 memory and our AdamW coupling, on seed 0:
  - the SGP baseline matches plain GPM on rollouts;
  - adaptive SGP keeps adaptive GPM's transition retention, but loses 20 pp of Object at the end;
  - it widens the T4 probe-loss gap instead of closing it (g = −0.63);
  - together with α = 25 (g = 0.36, with O1 failed), neither α run achieves SGP's aim here:
    recovering plasticity at saturation while keeping retention.
- **It does not show:**
  - **Robustness across seeds.** It is one seed, and seed 0 is the seed on which the sensitivity
    bracket was run. So α = 800 is not held out.
  - **What intermediate α would do.** α = 600 and 1000 were not run. The bracket was monotone in
    protection, and α = 25 and 800 lie on either side of the retention–plasticity trade-off, but that
    does not predict the intermediate outcomes.
  - **That scaled projection fails in general.** The mechanism points at the optimizer coupling,
    which is a choice of our AdamW adaptation: hard bases are removed before AdamW, and the scaling
    is applied after it. The paper's exact Adam-GP coupling, its annealed ε and the accumulation rule
    were not varied.

## 7. Disclosures

1. **How α was chosen.** α = 800 was picked as the middle of the 600–1000 range after the sensitivity
   bracket (seed 0) and the α = 400 validation (seeds 1–2) were known. It was not chosen by a
   registered rule.
2. **No smoke run at α = 800.** The code path is the registered α = 25 path, which was smoke-tested
   before the α = 25 runs, with a different constant. The only code change was the α-tagged run
   naming, covered by the CPU test suite before the commit.
3. **The durable flag is met by 1 pp** (64% against 63%), well inside rollout noise.
4. **Code commits.** The comparison runs come from earlier commits: G `0184293`, N `9701620`, and
   P25 and A25 `40cfdda`. The comparisons with α = 25 are descriptive, not paired contrasts in the
   report.

## 8. Open decisions (not automatic)

- **Close SGP as a characterisation.**
  - α controls protection monotonically.
  - Our AdamW adaptation does not recover plasticity at saturation.
  - α = 25 remains the registered main-table SGP baseline.
  - A methods note would cover the SGP–GPM coupling difference and the weight-decay no-op from the
    optimizer study.
- **Or one targeted test of the coupling instead of more α values:** a variant that changes only
  what AdamW sees, with the same memory and λ. This would be pre-registered and exploratory.
- **The deferred α = 600 / 1000 and seeds 1–2 at α = 800.** They are still possible (about 10 h per
  seed for both runs), but §5 gives little reason to expect a different outcome.
