# Gate 4 — flow-time (`s`) characterization over three seeds (no training)

**Status:** **FAIL** under the pre-registered rule.
- **Principal angles pass:** 79% of the same layers in all three seeds, against a required 50%.
- **`ρ_l(s)` variation fails:** 32%.
- **`c_l(s)` variation fails:** 12%.

Per README §9, **`s`-binned SGP is not built for the main table**; the null result and this
characterization are reported.

**Pre-registered interpretation:** failure does not establish that no layer contains
flow-time-dependent geometry. It means that broad, layerwise-reproducible evidence sufficient to
justify a general `s`-binned method was not obtained.

**In one sentence:** flow time changes *which* decoder directions are active, but not broadly *how
many* directions are needed, or how strongly the next task's gradients interfere with them.

## 1. What was measured
- **The layers.** The 34 registry layers whose activation inputs depend on `s`: every decoder layer
  except cross-attention K/V. The run asserts this count. `action_in` (d = 7) is the only
  small-`d_in` layer; it is flagged and kept in the denominator.
- **The captures.** For each seed, T1 was captured over the full dataset with `s ~ U(bin)`, in each
  of the 4 bins, in two replicates (A and B) that differ only in their `s` and `A_0` draws. There
  was also one pooled capture. The bases are taken at ε = 0.95.
- **The gradients.** T2 gradients, one full pass per bin, with common random numbers: identical
  batches, `A_0` and base uniforms. Each gradient was projected against `M_{b,A}`, `M_{b,B}` and the
  pooled basis.
- **The rule, per layer:**
  - (i) the replicate-mean ρ range must be at least `max(0.02, 3 × replicate noise)`;
  - (ii) the bin-0 vs bin-3 overlap, averaged over all four replicate pairs, must be at most the
    within-bin overlap minus 0.10;
  - (iii) the paired `c(bin 3) − c(bin 0)` must be at least 0.05 in absolute value, with a 95% CI
    excluding 0, for both basis replicates with the same sign.
- **The rule, per criterion:** at least 50% of the *same* layers must pass in all three seeds.

## 4. Reading
1. **Flow-time bins span measurably different input subspaces.**
   - Within a bin, the bases are almost identical between replicates (overlap about 0.99–1.00).
   - Between the extreme bins, the self-attention and MLP layers overlap only about 0.6–0.75.
   - This holds in 27 of 34 layers in every seed, with near-identical passing sets.
   - README §7.5 said "if the bins span the same subspace, the whole hypothesis dies". **They do
     not**; that part of the hypothesis survives.
2. **But the subspaces are of similar size.** Occupancy varies little: the median range is about
   0.02–0.03, at the floor. So ρ(s) variation is reproducible in only about a third of layers,
   even though the passing sets agree well (Jaccard up to 0.88).
3. **And the new task's gradients overlap every bin's subspace about equally.**
   - `c_l` is a gradient **projection-norm** ratio, `‖G_∥‖/‖G‖`, not an energy fraction. Against
     the bin's own T1 basis it is about 0.93 in every bin, which corresponds to an energy fraction
     of about `c² ≈ 0.87`.
   - The paired bin 0 vs bin 3 difference is small (median |Δc| ≤ 0.04) and not reproducible:
     only 4 layers pass in all seeds, with Jaccard 0.27–0.46.
   - A handful of layers show large but seed-specific effects, up to +0.29.
4. **Consequence for `s`-binned SGP.**
   - Per-bin projection would protect different directions in each bin.
   - It would not systematically change the gradient projection-norm fraction inside the protected
     subspace. The mechanism that would give `s`-binning its advantage, relaxing protection where
     per-bin interference is low, has no broad, reproducible support here.
   - Per the pre-registered rule and README §9, `sgp_sbinned` is dropped from the main
     experimental matrix. The thresholds are not relaxed.
   - `s`-binned protection for particular layers may be mentioned as future work, but not as a
     supported general method.

## 5. Caveats
1. **One curriculum and one task pair.** The run covers T1 bases and T2 gradients on `seq_hetero`,
   at the post-T1 checkpoint. Other pairs, later stages, or `seq_correlated` could differ.
2. **ε = 0.95 only.** The report stores ε ∈ {0.90, 0.95, 0.99} for the bases, but the rule reads
   0.95.
3. **The thresholds are judgements.** 0.02, 3η, 0.10, 0.05 and the 50% same-layer requirement were
   all chosen before the run. The rule is intentionally strict.
4. **Only the extreme bins decide (ii) and (iii).** The full overlap matrices for every bin pair are
   in the report.

## 6. Consequences for the roadmap
- **Drop `s`-binned SGP** as a main-table method. Report the Gate 4 null result, with the positive
  angle finding, as a characterization.
- **Adaptive GPM is the validated intervention so far.** The **next thesis-method candidate is
  adaptive SGP**, non-binned. It is the proposed response to adaptive GPM's T4 plasticity cost, and
  it has no results yet.
- **Next: pre-register the SGP runs.**
  - **Paper-faithful SGP** is the baseline.
  - **Adaptive SGP** is the candidate method.
  - Adaptive SGP gets **two separate objectives**: preserve the replicated Object retention effect,
    and recover the paired T4 plasticity loss relative to plain GPM.

  After that come the baselines and the Stage A matrix.
