# T5 saturation sweep — SGP α against hard projection from adaptive GPM's post-T4 state (seed 0): `none_passes`

**Status: exploratory, one seed, one new task, no claim level.** The pre-registered verdict is
**`none_passes`**: no tested α meets the plasticity–retention criteria on this T5 transition, so
the α sweep stops.
- **The replay gate passed bitwise.** Adaptive GPM's memory, rebuilt with SGP's update rule,
  equals the original in all 91 layers for all four tasks.
- **The premise holds.** Hard projection fits T5 worse than no projection: ln(L5_hard / L5_free) =
  **+0.92 [+0.75, +1.17]**, a ratio of ×2.5.
- **No α beats hard projection on T5 success.** Every paired T5 difference is between −16 and
  −2 pp, and no CI lies above 0.
- **No α is "effectively hard".** The `accumulation_limited` verdict does not apply.

**In short:**
- **What `none_passes` means.** Every α fails registered criterion (1): none gains ≥ 10 pp on T5
  over hard projection with a CI above 0 (the paired differences run from −16 to −2 pp). The
  verdict is a statement about that criterion only.
- **A separate observation, outside the rule: a retention–plasticity trade.** α = 400 and α = 800
  kept the old tasks better than hard projection:
  - mean old-task success 0.860 and 0.845, against hard's 0.795;
  - T3/T4 change from the start −8/−8 (α 400) and −8/−6 (α 800), against −16/−18 (hard);
  - at a lower T5: 64% and 70%, against 72%.

  The frozen rule selects on the T5 gain, so this trade selects nothing. It is reported (one seed,
  point estimates); it is not a selected configuration.
- **The coupling study's prediction held.** High α (400–1600) fits T5 **worse** than hard
  projection, with a 17–27% higher T5 probe loss. Low α fits it better in loss but forgets: at
  α = 25, T4 falls to 22%.
- **The saturation cost is detected in loss; in rollouts it is not separable from zero.** Hard
  reaches 72% on T5 against the free arm's 74%, at 2.5× the probe loss. Two points on one task
  and 50 episodes cannot show that the rollout cost is zero, nor that it is not.
- **Hard projection still erodes the two newest old tasks.** From the start state (98/84/94/92),
  T3 falls **−16 pp [−26, −6]** and T4 **−18 pp [−32, −6]**.
  - High-α SGP erodes them less (α = 400: −8 and −8; α = 800: −8 and −6) but learns T5 worse
    (64% and 70%).
  - This matches the earlier pattern, and it is *consistent with* the scaled directions taking
    Adam's step budget and shrinking the free-direction update (§5). This sweep does not test that
    mechanism: the arms differ in more than one respect.
- **SGP is closed** (`docs/thesis_plan.md`, A7). One seed and one task do not settle SGP in
  general. Within this thesis, no tested α (25–1600) under our AdamW adaptation converts
  saturation into usable plasticity on this transition.

## 1. Design (as pre-registered)

**The question.** Once the hard-projection memory is nearly full (adaptive GPM after T4: trunk
occupancy about 0.99), does switching to SGP (our AdamW adaptation) at some α learn a new task
better than hard projection while keeping T1–T4?

**The setup.** Every arm starts from the **same weights** (adaptive GPM seed 0, `stage3.pt`) and
the **same memory**, and trains only T5. This tests a **local, saturation-triggered GPM → SGP
switch**, not an SGP sequence from T1.
- **T5:** `libero_10/STUDY_SCENE1_pick_up_the_book_and_place_it_in_the_back_compartment_of_the_caddy`.
  It passed Gate 0 at 88% [78, 96] (`docs/runs/2026-10-01_gate0_t5.md`). Its descriptive
  plasticity threshold is 88 − 15 = **73%**.
- **Recipe:** adaptive GPM's own: 30k steps, lr 1e-4, AdamW, AMP, the §7.4 freeze (registry
  weights only).
- **Streams:** `derive_seed(seq_ft namespace, T5, 4)`, through the stage path with the runner's
  text-cache state.
- **Evaluation:** all five tasks, 50 paired episodes under the seq_ft namespace. T1–T4 are paired
  with adaptive GPM's stage-3 evaluation. Probe losses use the 16 fixed probe batches.

**Step 0, the replay (a gate).**
- **What it does.** Adaptive GPM's memory is rebuilt with SGP's update rule, stage by stage, from
  its own stage checkpoints, capture seeds and text-cache state (T1's instruction encoded under
  AMP, later ones in fp32). It records each task's σ′, so that λ(α) (Eq. 2/10) can be recomputed
  for any α.
- **The gate, per task and layer:** dimensions, ranks and `k_added`; residual spectra; the
  projector; the smallest principal-angle cosine; and the protected operator `Q = M Λ Mᵀ` for
  λ = 1 and every sweep α.
- **Every arm uses the replayed memory**, the hard arm included.

**The arms:**

| Arm | What it does |
|---|---|
| `free` | no projection; registry weights only |
| `hard` | GPM, λ = 1 |
| `sgp_a{25, 100, 400, 800, 1600}` | SGP at that α |

**The rule, per α against hard.** An α is **promising** only if all three hold:
1. a paired T5 success gain ≥ 10 pp, with a CI lower bound above 0;
2. ln(L5_α / L5_hard) with a CI upper bound below 0 (matched probe batches);
3. each of T1–T4 ≥ hard's − 10 pp (point estimates).

**The verdict, first match wins:**
1. `replay_invalid`
2. `incomplete`
3. `alpha_found`
4. `no_detected_saturation_cost` (the premise CI is not above 0)
5. `accumulation_limited` (none promising, and every α ≥ 100 is effectively hard)
6. `none_passes`

**Stated before the run** (config header, from `docs/runs/2026-10-01_sgp_coupling.md`): under
AdamW, high α (800, 1600) fits T5 *worse* than hard projection, and α = 25 fits it better but
forgets more.

## 5. Mechanism: measured, not causal

**How saturated the memory is (a design input, from T5's start Gram).** Only **0.14%** of T5's
trunk input energy and **0.31%** of its decoder input energy lie outside the replayed memory. Hard
projection therefore confines learning to directions that carry about 0.1–0.3% of the new task's
input energy.

**The importances λ(α)**, recomputed from the replay's σ′:

| α | trunk: share of hard bases / mean (1 − λ) | decoder: share of hard bases / mean (1 − λ) | nominal scaled pass-through, trunk / decoder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 0.915 / 0.025 | 0.507 / 0.297 | 0.0025 / 0.0112 |
| 100 | 0.955 / 0.008 | 0.675 / 0.148 | 0.00089 / 0.0036 |
| 400 | 0.955 / 0.0024 | 0.728 / 0.066 | 0.00026 / 0.0013 |
| 800 | 0.955 / 0.0012 | 0.728 / 0.040 | 0.00013 / 0.00075 |
| 1600 | 0.955 / 0.0006 | 0.728 / 0.022 | 0.00007 / 0.00040 |

- **"Effectively hard" means:** pass-through < 0.10 × the free fraction in **both** halves.
- At α = 1600 the trunk qualifies (7e-5 < 1.4e-4) but the decoder does not (4.0e-4 > 3.1e-4). So
  **no α** is effectively hard.
- At α = 25, the nominal scaled pass-through (0.25%) exceeds the trunk's whole free fraction
  (0.14%).

**The realized T5 update**, split against the replayed memory (norms; trunk / decoder):

| Arm | Total | Inside memory: hard | Inside memory: scaled | Free (outside) | Relative update | `update_c` (AdamW step inside memory) | raw `gradient_c` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| `hard` | 58.2 / 48.7 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | **58.2 / 48.7** | 0.385 | 0.39 / 0.27 | 0.998 / 0.996 |
| `free` | 40.9 / 27.1 | 40.8 / 25.1 | — | 3.0 / 10.3 | 0.249 | — | — |
| α 25 | 31.6 / 28.1 | 0 / 0 | 22.3 / 19.6 | 22.3 / 20.2 | 0.215 | 0.93 / 0.92 | 0.999 / 0.997 |
| α 100 | 33.5 / 32.3 | 0 / 0 | 13.4 / 17.1 | 30.7 / 27.4 | 0.236 | 0.91 / 0.89 | 0.998 / 0.997 |
| α 400 | 37.5 / 35.3 | 0 / 0 | 4.8 / 10.1 | 37.2 / 33.8 | 0.261 | 0.89 / 0.87 | 0.998 / 0.996 |
| α 800 | 39.0 / 36.4 | 0 / 0 | 2.6 / 6.4 | 38.9 / 35.9 | 0.271 | 0.88 / 0.86 | 0.997 / 0.995 |
| α 1600 | 40.0 / 37.4 | 0 / 0 | 1.4 / 3.8 | 39.9 / 37.2 | 0.278 | 0.87 / 0.86 | 0.997 / 0.995 |

1. **Unconstrained, T5's learning goes almost entirely into memory directions.** The free arm's
   trunk update lies 99.7% inside memory by norm (40.8 of 40.9). This is the premise seen from the
   update side, and it matches the coupling study's unprojected AdamW arm at T4.
2. **Hard projection pushes a large update through a tiny free subspace.** Its trunk update
   (58.2) is larger than the free arm's whole update (40.9). It fits T5 to 72% at 2.5× the free
   arm's probe loss.
3. **Under SGP, the scaled directions take Adam's step budget.**
   - AdamW's raw step lies 87–93% inside memory (hard: 27–39%), and the post-step scaling removes
     most of it.
   - The free component grows with α (trunk 22.3 → 39.9) but stays below hard's 58.2 at every α.
   - This is consistent with the coupling study (`docs/runs/2026-10-01_sgp_coupling.md` §7).
4. **More free-direction movement goes with more erosion of the newest old tasks.** Hard has the
   largest free update and loses 16–18 pp on T3/T4. α = 400–800 move less and lose 6–8 pp. This is
   associative, across arms that differ in more than one respect. It suggests the old tasks are not
   fully protected inside the "free" directions, which is consistent with the adaptive rule leaving
   10% of each later task's new energy unprotected. It is not a tested mechanism.

## 6. What this does and does not show

**It shows**, for one seed and one new task, from adaptive GPM's saturated post-T4 state, under
our AdamW adaptation:
- switching locally to SGP at any tested α does not learn T5 better than hard projection in
  rollouts;
- high α fits T5 worse but erodes the newest old tasks less;
- low α fits T5 better in loss but erodes T4 heavily;
- the saturation cost is detected at the loss level (×2.5); in T5 rollouts it is not separable
  from zero (72 against 74%, one task);
- at T5, retention under hard projection is also at stake: T3 −16 and T4 −18 pp.

**It does not show:**
- anything about an SGP sequence trained from T1 (this is a local switch at saturation);
- a coupling-aware SGP variant, or exact SGD (`sgp_coupling` found exact SGD impractical here);
- other seeds, other T5 tasks, or longer sequences;
- that the T5 rollout cost of saturation is zero. 72 against 74% is one task, and the free arm
  itself is 14 pp below Gate 0.

## 8. Consequences

- **SGP is closed** as a characterization, per `docs/thesis_plan.md` (A7).
  - α controls protection monotonically.
  - Under our AdamW adaptation, the scaled directions take Adam's step budget. This holds at T1→T2
    (sensitivity study), at T4 (coupling study) and now at saturation (T5).
  - α = 25 stays the registered main-table SGP baseline.
  - **No further SGP studies.**
- **For the main bet (`docs/thesis_plan.md`, Workstream E):**
  - This sweep's `hard` and `free` arms are the **matched-start reference pair** for the T5
    two-sided pilot (E2). The reference gap is 2.51× with a CI above 0, so the gap-closure
    criterion applies.
  - The 0.14% / 0.31% free-energy fractions quantify how saturated the input-side memory is.
  - The erosion of T3/T4 under hard projection means the 8-task evaluation (E3) must weigh
    retention, not only plasticity.
- **Methods notes** (`docs/methods_notes.md`): the SGP–Adam step-budget coupling now has three
  measurement points (T2, T4, T5).
